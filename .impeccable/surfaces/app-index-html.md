# כדורגל טריוויה

Mode: operate. Mobile web, Hebrew RTL, one phone.

Audience: Israeli friends playing a ten-question round about 90s and early-2000s football. Task: pick an era and a mode, answer, see the score. Proof is the existing questions, careers, and personal bests. Untouched: game rules, scoring, timers, data.js.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A current match-night app. The name and the score are a condensed Hebrew display face in floodlight lime, on a night ground, with the question on a paper sheet. It refuses the emoji pitch app and the vintage broadcast strap.

OWN-WORLD: Night ink #101114, match paper #F4F2EC, floodlight lime #D6F25C for the live signal and the forward button. Karantina for the name and the figures, Rubik for questions and controls. One offset shadow on the question sheet. Correct and wrong use a drawn mark as well as color.

STORY: Friends recognize the game, choose a round, answer ten questions, and leave with a score and a personal best.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390-wide night. כדורגל in paper Karantina, טריוויה in lime under it, tagline in Rubik, no label above the name. A pill era control, selected half lime. A hairline list of three modes, best score in Karantina.
